# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Compact, sortable list that replaces the Looking For Group browser
- Class icons and role badges for every group member
- Filters for activities, groups or singles, needed role and playstyle, plus a search box
- My roles quick filter
- Whisper and Invite buttons on every row
- Whisper marker that turns green when the leader replies
- Gold highlight on new listings, and automatic refresh every 30 seconds
- Zone column and level colors compared to your own level
- Hide players from the list
- My Listing page to list yourself or your group from inside GroupGlance
- Native Blizzard look, movable and resizable window
- Minimap button
